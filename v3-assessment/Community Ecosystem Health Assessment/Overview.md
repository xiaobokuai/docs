# Community Ecosystem Health Assessment

Definition: Evaluating the overall health of open source communities, including community vitality, collaboration efficiency, and governance openness, reflecting the community's sustainable development capability.

# Assessment Model

The community ecosystem health assessment includes three key dimensions:

## Community Vitality

Evaluating the community's activity level and attractiveness, including community popularity, contribution activity, and developer base.

For detailed metrics, please refer to: [Community Vitality](./Community%20Vitality.md)

## Collaboration Efficiency

Evaluating the community's collaboration quality and response efficiency, including response timeliness and collaboration development quality.

For detailed metrics, please refer to: [Collaboration Efficiency](./Collaboration%20Efficiency.md)

## Open Governance

Evaluating the community's governance openness and inclusiveness, including organizational open governance and individual open governance.

For detailed metrics, please refer to: [Open Governance](./Open%20Governance.md)

# Assessment Significance

Community ecosystem health is the foundation for the sustainable development of open source projects. A healthy open source community should possess:
- Active community participation and continuous contribution inflow
- Efficient collaboration mechanisms and good response speed
- Open and transparent governance structure and diverse participants

Through community ecosystem health assessment, communities can identify their strengths and weaknesses in community building, formulate targeted improvement strategies, and promote the long-term healthy development of the community.
